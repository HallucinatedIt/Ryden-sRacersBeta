How to play
W / ↑Accelerate
S / ↓Brake · reverse when stopped
AD / ←→Steer
SpaceHop & drift. Hold while steering through a corner — sparks go blue → orange → pink. Release for a drift boost.
Shift / EUse item
RReset car to the track
Esc / PPause
TouchLeft thumb anywhere on the left side is a joystick: slide to steer, pull down to brake/reverse. Right side: DRIFT, ITEM, BRAKE. Gas is automatic unless you switch Auto gas off in Settings; then rest your thumb between GAS and DRIFT to press both. You can slide your thumb between buttons.
GamepadRT / A accelerate · LT / B brake · stick steer · RB / X drift · LB / Y item · Back reset · Start pause

Prism crystals give an item: ⚡ Nitro Cell (big boost), ◈ Aegis Bubble (shield — blocks one hazard and bumps rivals aside), ◍ Glaze Slick (drop a sticky puddle behind you that spins out whoever hits it).

Chevron pads on the road give a free boost. Hold accelerate just before GO! for a rocket start. Staying off the shoulders keeps your speed up; jumps need speed to clear the gaps.
